HOW TO PLAY

1
Tap to Drop
Blocks slide back and forth across alternating axes. Tap, click, or press Spacebar to drop the block.
2
Precision Slicing
Any part of the block that overhangs the tower gets sliced off! The remaining platform becomes the base for the next level.
3
Perfect Placement Combos
Align the block perfectly to hear ascending musical chime notes! Hit 5 combos in a row to RECOVER and expand your platform width!
4
Special Events & Surprises
Keep stacking to trigger Slow Motion Focus Time, Double Points, and Golden Blocks!
