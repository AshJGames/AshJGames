📖 How to Play

1. The Golden Rule

Read the instruction carefully before tapping. A single wrong tap or running out of time immediately ends your game!

2. Negative Commands

"DON'T tap Blue" or "Tap anything except Green" means any other color button is valid. Avoid the forbidden color!

3. Stroop Effect & Tricks

If instructed to tap the INK COLOR, look at what color the font glows in, not what word is written!

4. Combos & Speed Bonus

Fast taps (under 350ms) grant extra speed points. Streaks give combo multipliers up to x4.0, plus bonus points every 5 streak!

5. Game Modes

Classic: 30s endurance run.
Chaos: Shuffling buttons & fake glitch instructions.
Impossible: Brutal sub-second reverse brain-benders.
Daily: Compete on the same seed as the world!
